About BaseTen

BaseTen is an independent, student-led educational initiative founded and developed by Nishad Kulkarni, a rising junior at Argyle High School and aspiring computer science major. The project is designed, built, and maintained by a single author, who serves as the founder, developer, and sole instructor. BaseTen operates as a free, supplemental platform for math and technology instruction.

Background

This project is inspired by academic preparation in math and computer science. The courses taken include math all the way up to Algebra II, as well as AP Computer Science Principles and AP Computer Science A. Experience in competitions includes UIL Number Senses, reaching the district level for Argyle High School and coming in 8th place in the Texas Math and Science Coaches Association Number Sense contest. Programming skills in particular include Java and Python, and some knowledge of HTML and CSS for web development.

Role and Responsibilities

    All aspects of BaseTen are independently managed, including:
  • Curriculum design and lesson structuring
  • Instructional content creation and recording
  • Website development and deployment
  • Platform maintenance and iteration
  • Documentation of instructional scope and progress

How BaseTen Works

    BaseTen is intended as a free educational resource and does not offer formal certification or accreditation. Instructional content is limited to foundational mathematics, digital literacy, and introductory programming topics. The project is developed iteratively, with content expanded as lessons are delivered and refined based on deployment experience.
