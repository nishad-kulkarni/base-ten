Welcome to BaseTen!

What is BaseTen?

BaseTen is an independent educational initiative providing structured instruction in math and technology to underprivileged students in India through accessible, low-bandwidth online content.

Our Mission

We believe that every student deserves access to quality education, regardless of their background or circumstances. Our mission is to bridge the gap in educational opportunities by providing free, high-quality resources to students who need them most.

What Students Learn

  • Mental math strategies drawn from competitive Number Sense
  • Foundational digital literacy using Google Workspace tools
  • Introductory programming concepts in Python and Java
  • Core computational thinking aligned with AP CSP and AP CSA

How BaseTen Works

  • Short recorded lectures focused on a single concept
  • Guided examples followed by independent practice
  • Asynchronous access designed for low-bandwidth environments
  • Self-paced progression emphasizing mastery
